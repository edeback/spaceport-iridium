Services for ships
Give reasons for ships to dock. Docked ships have passengers that come aboard to use services. Ships also buy Ship Fuel and Spare Parts - automatically if available, or minor alert if they are demanded but unavailable.
Category: Docks? Transportation? External Connections? (teleporter might go here)
Docking Bay - Small trade ships, small passenger shuttles ("Allows a single ship with goods or passengers")
Refueling Depot - allows nearby docking bays to sell ship fuel
Ship Repair Yard - allows nearby docking bays to repair ships using spare parts
Shuttle Bay - Contains shuttles belonging to the station. Opens external expeditions (with other modules, research)
Cargo Terminal - Large trade ships
Passenger Terminal - Large passenger ships, the cruiseliners of space
- Upgrades: Disease screening
When trading goods with trade ships, they'll take from _any_ docking bay (or else moving them around gets super complicated?), but they'll deposit to whatever bay they're in

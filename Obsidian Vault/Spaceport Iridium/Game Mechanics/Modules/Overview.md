

Support Structure (Substructure?)
- Required(?)) foundation for other modules
- Empty support is very low cost to maintain
- SS is removed when a module is built over it
- SS is added when a module is removed

Movement:

Hallway
- Empty but habitable space that people can walk through
- Low cost to maintain

Promenade
- Double-level hallway, effectively
- Can get people between floors without tubes/lifts

Service Tube
- Basically a ladder between floors
- High movement penalty
- Low cost

Turbolift
- Point-to-point anywhere vertically/horizontally/diagonally
- Multiple lifts (turbocars) can be in one turbolift shaft

Teleporter
- Point-to-point instant travel
- Very high energy requirements

External Connections:

Shuttle Docking Bay
- Allows a number of shuttles to dock/undock
- Station starts with one

Docking Ring/Pylon
- Allows larger ships to dock/undock

Energy:

Solar panel
- Requires no fuel
- Output dependent on sunlight incidence
- Station starts with two

Fusion reactor
- Requires hydrogen fuel

Mining/Processing:

Storeroom
- Holds specific amount of resources
- Can be general or set up to be specific

Entertainment/Social:

Promenade
- Main public thoroughfare in which visitors and residents congregate

Holodeck

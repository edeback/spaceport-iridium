Hallway
- Empty but habitable space that people can walk through
- Low cost to maintain

Promenade
- Double-level hallway, effectively
- Can get people between floors without tubes/lifts

Service Tube
- Basically a ladder between floors
- High movement penalty
- Low cost

Turbolift
- Point-to-point anywhere vertically/horizontally/diagonally
- Multiple lifts (turbocars) can be in one turbolift shaft

Teleporter
- Point-to-point instant travel
- Very high energy requirements

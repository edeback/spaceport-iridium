
"Hallway and Shafts" layer vs "everything else"

You build out hallways and shafts (ladders to other levels) first and then attach modules to those. You can also build out structural elements that you can also attach modules to, though units can't get to them without hallways.

There are airlocks that allow units to go outside the hallways into space. This is to allow them to build modules that aren't otherwise connected.

Hallways/shafts are on the closest z-plane. Modules are on the next z-plane. Structural elements are on the back z-plane (maybe?)

Anything that is not a hallway/turbolift is a module, 

Units always exit a module into a hallway. The door(s?) on modules are clearly marked when placing them.

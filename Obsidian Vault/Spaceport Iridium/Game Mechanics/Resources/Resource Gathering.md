
Drone Bay
- Where drones are stored
- Has good amount of storage space for ore/ice/gas

Mining Drone
- Leaves station, mines asteroid/comet/gas cloud, returns with resources
- Can prioritize what sort of resource gathered
- Can be specialized (ore/ice/gas)
- Can be upgraded (faster mining, more efficient mining, larger cargo space)

Ore/Ice/Gas
- Should have quality levels, or otherwise description of contents
	- e.g. ore that is "10% metals, 15% carbon, 20% silicates, 3% precious metals, 1% iridium, 30% water"

Large Mining Drone
- Requires Large Drone Bay
- Basically better in all respects than Mining Drone

Station-based Magscoop
- Attached directly as a module
- Very slowly gathers gas ions (mostly hydrogen) based on solar wind
- Can upgrade but generally very inefficient

Matter Synthesis
- Direct energy -> matter conversion
- EXTREMELY inefficient, basically anything else is better
	- Should be net negative if trying to create hydrogen to put back into fusion reactor

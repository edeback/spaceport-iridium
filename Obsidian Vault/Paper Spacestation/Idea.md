A modified Spaceport Iridium Board Game -> video game conversion, single player
Must build up your spacestation with the goal of defeating pirate enemies as they spawn, plus final boss battle
Rogue-lite: available modules are randomized in terms of order and availability, same with resources and pirate spawns
"Paper" aesthetic, to make images easy to make as well as to reinforce the boardgame-like play style
Real-time-with-pause: Actions happen on timers, but you can queue up actions during a pause
Worker placement: Drones automatically execute orders
- Could have global or drone/based list of orders
- Could have priorities on different jobs and let drones auto-pick
